## Platform

web

## Users

A local PEMBINA organizer tracking the development of mad'u individuals through Mutabaah, Muhib, and Muayyid activities, follow-up, and development work.

## Product Purpose

Kit PD tracks individual progress and provides references for planning mad'u development.

## Operating Context

The product supports local PEMBINA organizing work across individual records, activity and follow-up tracking, development planning, and related operational views. The current implementation is a single-page web app with spreadsheet import support.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Track individuals through Mutabaah, Muhib, and Muayyid development stages.
- Record activities, follow-up, and development work.
- Provide references to support development planning.
- Privacy and data handling are durable constraints; future work must treat member and mad'u information as sensitive.
- Product terminology includes PEMBINA, mad'u, Mutabaah, Muhib, and Muayyid.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: Kit PD.
- Organizational context: PEMBINA Shah Alam.

- Connect tracking with practical follow-up and planning.
